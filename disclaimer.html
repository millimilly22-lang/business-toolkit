Always verify important business information against the original document.

OCR and extraction accuracy

OCR can misread text, numbers, dates, invoice numbers, tax identifiers, bank details and totals. Missing-field checks may incorrectly mark information as present or missing.

Spreadsheet and PDF analysis

Calculated totals, duplicate detection, missing-value checks and file summaries depend on the source data and browser processing. They may not identify every issue or interpret every document format correctly.

Invoices and VAT

Invoice and VAT tools are convenience features. Tax rates, invoice requirements and accounting rules differ by transaction and jurisdiction. Verify the final invoice before sending, filing or relying on it.

No professional advice

Business Toolkit does not provide legal, accounting, tax, investment or financial advice. Consult a qualified professional where professional advice is needed.

Data preservation

Browser-stored data can be lost. Keep separate backups of important invoices, receipts, reports and business records.

Third-party services

The website depends on hosting and third-party browser libraries. Business Toolkit cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability or the continued availability of those external components.
