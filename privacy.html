Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the current Business Toolkit public beta handles information when you use the website.

1. Current service model

The current version does not require a Business Toolkit user account, paid subscription or API key. Core invoice, expense and saved-document records are designed to remain in your browser storage on the device you use.

2. Information stored locally in your browser

The app may store invoice records, expense records, document-analysis results and related settings in browser local storage. This data is tied to that browser/device and may be removed if you clear browser data, use private browsing, uninstall the browser or reach storage limits.

3. Files you choose to analyze

When you select a spreadsheet, image or PDF, the website processes that file in the browser for the requested feature. OCR and PDF/Excel libraries run in the browser. You should only process documents you are authorized to use.

4. OCR and document text

Image OCR is performed through browser-loaded software. Extracted text may be temporarily held in memory and, if you choose to save the analysis, selected results may be stored locally in your browser. Business Toolkit does not intentionally upload your document contents to its own application server in the current beta.

5. Technical hosting data

The hosting provider may automatically process ordinary technical request information needed to deliver the website, such as IP address, request time, browser/device information, requested URL and security or diagnostic logs. This information is controlled by the relevant infrastructure provider under its own terms and policies.

6. Third-party browser libraries

The website currently loads browser libraries for spreadsheet reading, PDF processing and OCR from third-party content delivery networks. When your browser requests these library files, those providers may receive normal network information such as your IP address, user agent and request metadata. Business Toolkit does not intentionally send your spreadsheet, image or PDF contents to those CDN providers.

7. Cookies and similar storage

Business Toolkit does not currently use advertising cookies or a Business Toolkit analytics account. The app uses browser local storage for core records. Third-party infrastructure or libraries may use their own technical mechanisms according to their policies.

8. Purpose of processing

Information is processed to provide the requested tools, operate and secure the website, troubleshoot service problems and preserve locally saved records where you choose to save them.

9. Retention

Locally stored records remain until you delete them, clear browser storage or the browser/device removes them. Hosting and infrastructure logs, if created, are retained according to the relevant provider's retention practices.

10. Data sharing

Business Toolkit does not sell user document contents or browser-stored business records. Technical data may be processed by hosting and content-delivery providers as necessary to serve the website and browser libraries.

11. International processing

Infrastructure and CDN providers may process technical data in different countries. Their own privacy notices explain their locations, safeguards and transfer mechanisms.

12. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used for the website, but no browser, device or internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not use the beta as the only copy of important business records.

13. Your choices and rights

You can remove locally stored Business Toolkit data by deleting records in the app or clearing site data in your browser. Depending on applicable law and the kind of information involved, you may also have privacy rights relating to personal data processed by a service operator or infrastructure provider.

14. Children

Business Toolkit is intended as a business utility and is not designed for use by children as a consumer service.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before features such as user accounts, cloud document storage, payments, first-party analytics, advertising or server-side document processing are introduced.

16. Operator and privacy contact

Current beta status: no paid checkout or account registration is offered.

A real operator identity and privacy/support contact must be published before commercial account or payment features are launched. Business Toolkit does not invent or publish personal contact details that the operator has not provided.
