Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Business Toolkit is designed so its core invoice, expense and document records are stored locally in your browser. The website does not require an account or API key for its core features.

Local data

Invoices, expense records and saved document scans may be stored using browser storage on the device you use. Clearing browser data can remove these records. You should export or back up important business information.

Uploaded files

Spreadsheet and image files selected in the app are processed in your browser for the features provided by the current version. Do not upload information you do not have permission to process.

Third-party libraries

The spreadsheet analyzer may load a browser library from a content-delivery network to read Excel files. Your own spreadsheet content is processed by the app in the browser and is not intentionally sent to that library provider by Business Toolkit.

Future services

If cloud sync, accounts, payments, analytics, OCR or other online services are added later, this policy should be updated before those features are released.

Contact

A public contact method should be added here before a commercial launch.
